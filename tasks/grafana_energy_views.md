# Grafana dashboards rework + energy views on the web

Repo UHARPI (read UHA_FW `UHA_COMMON/VarDefs/uha_vars.yaml` and **`UHA_COMMON/VarDefs/ENERGY_TOPOLOGY.md`** — the
energy topology, derived values and the consumption-breakdown rules; read it first). Lightweight mode: implement,
regenerate, deploy, check in the browser, short report. Branch `feature/grafana-energy-views`.

Dashboards are generated by `UHA/Grafana/make_dashboards.py` (deploy `deploy_grafana.sh`); never edit the JSON by
hand. **Grafana stays English.** Labels: English short names from `WS/i18n/texts.json` (`vars.<NAME>.en_short`,
fall back to `en`, then the name); chart-specific labels below may override. Titles in English, no YAML names in
legends or titles anywhere. Colors as on the web: production/solar green `#28a745`, battery yellow `#ffc107`,
consumption/load red `#dc3545` (other series free, but distinct).

Rules from Martin (keep them):
- Two views, **never mixed or summed**: consumption breakdown (loads/circuits) vs. inverters (overload, lifetime).
- Group by meaning, never by board/node.

## Part A — Dashboard "UHA Energy" (top to bottom)

1. **Power** (as now): solar green, battery yellow, load red; legend "Solar", "Battery", "Load".
2. **State of charge**: `BAT_SOC` as "Total", thick yellow line (width 3); packs thin (width 1), colors free, legend
   "BMS1 251 Ah", "BMS2 220 Ah", "BMS3 80 Ah" (no "SOC" in the labels).
3. **Energy per day (last 30 days)** — Grafana **Bar chart** panel (not time series) so the bars are **side by side**
   per day: "Production" (green, `LPV1_PROD_WH + LPV2_PROD_WH`), "Consumption" (red, `LLOAD1_CONS_WH +
   LLOAD2_CONS_WH`), "Balance" = production − consumption (yellow, hidden by default, switchable from the legend).
   x axis = local date. Delete the "Energy per hour" panel.
4. **Loads per day (last 30 days)** (was "Meters per day"), stacked bars, **no inverter meters** in it. Categories
   (see ENERGY_TOPOLOGY.md "Consumption breakdown"): Fridge, Kitchen (`CONS_KITCHEN_WH`, = A+B summed in IOBOARD_D),
   Washing machine, Technical room, Other circuits (`CONS_OTHER_WH`), Workshop (`CONS_AXPERT_WH − CONS_EVSE_WH`),
   EV charger (`CONS_EVSE_WH`), Workshop air conditioner (`CONS_WS_HEATING_WH`), Water heater (`EL_HEATER_CONS_WH`),
   Axpert losses (∫`AXPERT_LOAD_W` − `CONS_AXPERT_WH`; integrate the logged average power, **do not use
   `AXPERT_ENERGY_TODAY_WH`** — it is not monotonic and the logger's delta reset rule mangles it), and **"Other"**
   = daily consumption (same as in panel 3) − all the categories above (lights, DC loads, inverter losses, kids room
   + office, errors; may be negative on bad days — show it, do not clamp). The stack must add up to the
   consumption bar of panel 3. One place in the generator lists the categories (later "Other" will be split).
5. **Inverters power**: 300 W, 3 kW, 5 kW, Axpert (`POW_AXPERT_W`), Victron (`POW_WS_HEATING_W`, the only load on
   it today). Inverter view only.
6. **Loads power**: the same categories as panel 4 as power: circuit meters, Workshop (`POW_AXPERT_W − POW_EVSE_W`),
   EV charger, air conditioner, Water heater (`EL_HEATER_CURRENT_A × STRG1_VOLTAGE_V`), "Other" = `LOAD_W` − all
   above (will be noisy: sources refresh at different rates; accepted). Stacked area or lines — choose what reads
   better, say which.
7. **Storages power** (moved to the bottom): house storage, workshop storage, house/workshop local PV and load with
   readable labels, plus **LVDC link** estimate: `(BMS1_CURRENT_A − AXPERT_BAT_CURRENT_A) × STRG2_VOLTAGE_V`, positive
   = house → workshop. **Verify the signs on data** first (both currents should be positive when charging; check a
   sunny and a night period) and state in the panel description that it is an estimate (workshop DC loads neglected).

Grafana-side SQL only (no UhaGatePy change). If a computed series needs a new SQL helper, keep it small and
commented like the existing ones.
